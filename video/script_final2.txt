SCENE 1 (0-10s) — Hook
Your attention is leaking, and nobody is measuring it. MindLoop fixes that: get your baseline done, then grow it — game by game.

SCENE 2 (10-20s) — Problem
Brain games promise higher IQ. Screen-time dashboards show hours but explain nothing. Nobody connects how you play, sleep, scroll, and feel into one personal picture.

SCENE 3 (20-30s) — Assessment
MindLoop starts with a real assessment. Four games, picked by science. Vigilance Tap for attention span. Remember the Shelf for visual memory. Schulte 25 for scanning speed. Plus a habits quiz. Ten minutes, and your starting line is drawn.

SCENE 4 (30-40s) — Games
Then thirty research-grounded games. Spot the rebel among fifty orbiting balls. Tap one to twenty-five against the clock. Hold your phone perfectly still. Every tap feeds the same personal baseline.

SCENE 5 (40-50s) — Baseline
That baseline is the heart of MindLoop. Zero to one hundred, compared only with your own usual pattern. Never a label, never a diagnosis. Just you versus yesterday's you.

SCENE 6 (50-60s) — Buddy plan
Every day, your buddy plan: five games plus one small habit. Miss a day? Totally okay — your buddy just says try tomorrow. No streaks lost, no shame.

SCENE 7 (60-70s) — Screen context
Screen time and doomscrolling sit next to your focus scores — as context, never judgement. When scores dip, you'll see why: less sleep, more scrolling, or just a tired day.

SCENE 8 (70-80s) — Story report
One button generates your story report — coordination, attention, focus, written so simply a ten-year-old understands it. Plus five concrete ways to grow stronger.

SCENE 9 (80-90s) — Safety
Built responsibly: safety flags route to human review, referrals tracked till closure, every recommendation shows its reasoning. Twenty languages, private by design.

SCENE 10 (90-100s) — Evidence
No hype: training shows near-transfer, not miracle IQ gains — and we say so openly. Validated scales, long follow-up windows, a model card anyone can read.

SCENE 11 (100-110s) — CTA
Fill a thirty-second form, play four games, watch your baseline come alive. Then grow it, one small day at a time.

SCENE 12 (110-120s) — Close
MindLoop — know your focus, from your own data.
