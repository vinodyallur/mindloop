SCENE 1 (0-10s) — Welcome
Hey! Welcome to MindLoop. Here's the deal: first you get your baseline done with a few fun games. Then, day by day, you grow your attention span, sharpen your focus — and spend a little less time doomscrolling.

SCENE 2 (10-20s) — Who are you
Step one: who are you? Just your name, age, gender, and country. Thirty seconds, that's it. This makes your baseline truly yours — because a sixteen-year-old and a forty-year-old shouldn't be measured the same way.

SCENE 3 (20-30s) — Baseline games
Step two: play your four baseline games. Vigilance Tap measures pure attention span. Remember the Shelf tests what you remember seeing. N-Back trains working memory. And Hold Steady checks mind-body coordination in thirty seconds. That's your starting line.

SCENE 4 (30-40s) — Devices
Step three: connect your devices. Your smartwatch shares sleep and heart-rate context. Your phone shares screen-time context. Neither measures attention directly — they tell the story around your scores.

SCENE 5 (40-50s) — Screen time
Step four: log yesterday's screen time. Rough numbers are fine. If heavy doomscrolling days sit next to lower focus scores, you'll see it — as context, never as judgement.

SCENE 6 (50-60s) — Buddy plan
Now the fun part: your buddy plan. Five games plus one small habit, every day. Day one is simple — read a book for fifteen minutes, phone face-down. Small steps, every day, beat heroic efforts once a month.

SCENE 7 (60-70s) — Shelf game
Take Remember the Shelf. Six objects, twenty seconds to memorize them. Then tap only what you saw. Green means you remembered — red means sneaky intruder! It's memory training disguised as a game.

SCENE 8 (70-80s) — Hold Steady
Or try Hold Steady. Just hold your phone flat for thirty seconds. Your hand's tiny wobbles reveal how calm and coordinated your brain and body are right now. Tired hands wobble more — rest fixes it.

SCENE 9 (80-90s) — Missed day
Missed a day? Totally okay. Your buddy just says: try tomorrow. No streaks lost, no shame, no guilt-trip notifications. The only rule that matters is: never miss twice.

SCENE 10 (90-100s) — Story report
Then press one button — Generate my report — and MindLoop reads all your games and tells you, like a storybook, how your coordination, attention span, and focus are doing. Written so simply, a ten-year-old understands it. Plus five concrete ways to grow stronger.

SCENE 11 (100-110s) — Baseline index
Behind the story is real math: a zero-to-one-hundred index compared only with your own usual pattern. No population norms, no labels, never a diagnosis. Just you, versus yesterday's you.

SCENE 12 (110-120s) — CTA
So — get your baseline done, then grow it. MindLoop: know your focus, from your own data. See you on the leaderboard of one: yourself!
