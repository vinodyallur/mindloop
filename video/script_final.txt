SCENE 1 (0-10s) — Hook
Your attention is leaking, and nobody is measuring it. Endless scrolling trains your brain to flit, never to focus. MindLoop fixes that: get your baseline done, then grow it — game by game.

SCENE 2 (10-20s) — Problem
Here's the problem. Brain games promise higher IQ. Screen-time dashboards show hours but explain nothing. And a single test score means nothing without knowing YOUR usual. Nobody connects how you play, sleep, scroll, and feel — into one personal picture.

SCENE 3 (20-30s) — Assessment
So MindLoop starts with a real assessment. Four games, picked by science. Vigilance Tap measures pure attention span. Remember the Shelf tests visual memory. Schulte 25 clocks your scanning speed. And a habits quiz captures your everyday focus patterns. Ten minutes, and your starting line is drawn.

SCENE 4 (30-40s) — Games
Then comes the fun part: thirty research-grounded games. Spot the rebel among fifty orbiting balls. Tap numbers one to twenty-five against the clock. Hold your phone perfectly still for thirty seconds. Every tap, every reaction time, feeds the same personal baseline.

SCENE 5 (40-50s) — Baseline
That baseline is the heart of MindLoop. A zero-to-one-hundred index compared only with your own usual pattern — never with population norms, never a label, never a diagnosis. Just you versus yesterday's you, with a forecast of where you're heading.

SCENE 6 (50-60s) — Buddy plan
Every day, your buddy plan appears: five games plus one small habit — read fifteen minutes, walk ten, breathe for two. Miss a day? Totally okay. Your buddy just says: try tomorrow. No streaks lost, no shame, no guilt-trip notifications.

SCENE 7 (60-70s) — Screen context
Meanwhile MindLoop watches the full picture. Your screen time and doomscrolling sit next to your focus scores — as context, never judgement. Sleep, mood, and activity complete the story. When scores dip, you'll see why: less sleep, more scrolling, or just a tired day.

SCENE 8 (70-80s) — Story report
Then press one button: Generate my report. MindLoop reads all your games and tells you, like a storybook, how your coordination, attention span, and focus are doing — written so simply a ten-year-old understands it. Plus five concrete ways to grow stronger.

SCENE 9 (80-90s) — Safety
And it's built responsibly. Safety flags route to human review. Referrals are tracked until formally closed, with hospital-ready exports. Every recommendation shows its reasoning. Twenty languages, private by design, honest about limits.

SCENE 10 (90-100s) — Evidence
We don't hype. Training evidence shows near-transfer to similar tasks, not miracle IQ gains — and we say so openly. Validated scales, thirty, ninety, and hundred-eighty-day windows, and a model card anyone can read. Proof over promises.

SCENE 11 (100-110s) — CTA
So: fill a thirty-second form, play four games, and watch your baseline come alive. Then grow it, one small day at a time.

SCENE 12 (110-120s) — Close
MindLoop — know your focus, from your own data.
